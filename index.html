Why NixOS?

NixOS is declarative and reproducible.

Projects like Omarchy have the right idea: let people describe what they want instead of manually configuring everything.

But on Arch, that means adding an abstraction layer to an operating system that was never designed to work this way.

NixOS already is.

Packages, services, desktop configuration and much of your system state can already be described as code. That makes NixOS unusually well suited to an AI agent.
